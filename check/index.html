Vet every quote in your presentation

Paste every quote from your slides, paper, or bibliography — one per line — and check them all in one pass. We’ll flag the misattributed and unconfirmed ones so nothing embarrassing makes it in front of an audience. Nothing about your deck is stored.

up to 100 at once — nothing is stored

How this works: your text is matched (in your browser) against the 1098 quotes we’ve traced to a primary source. A match returns our verdict, the correct credit, and reuse status; no match means we haven’t verified that exact line — which isn’t proof it’s fake, only that you shouldn’t present it as confirmed. Agents can call the same data at /verify-index.json, the /verify API, or POST a whole deck to /verify-batch.

Reuse status is our research, not legal advice — and copyright varies by country and edition. This page is the fastest way to catch a misattribution before you publish; for commercial or published use, confirm rights with the rightsholder. See terms.
