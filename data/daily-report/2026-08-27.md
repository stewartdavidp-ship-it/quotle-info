### 4.1 The merge authority missed three consecutive slots *(decision 40 — NEW, and the only urgent one)*

`merge.yml` is on `cron: '0 */3 * * *'` — eight passes a day. Its own header explains why eight and
not one:

> Eight passes a day clears the Monday double and absorbs a missed run without a human noticing.

That absorption is what has been spent. Runs expected at 03:00, 06:00 and 09:00 UTC did not happen,
and 12:00 had not appeared by 12:35 UTC. For comparison, by this hour yesterday the pass had run four
times (01:15, 03:53, 06:48, 09:41 UTC).

Two of the three misses cost nothing — at 03:00 and 06:00 the only open PR was #669, which the pass
does not merge on its own day. **The 09:00 miss is the one that bit**: #670, #671 and #672 were all
open, ready and green by 09:07 UTC.

This is self-healing *if the schedule resumes* — the 15:00 UTC pass would clear all four PRs, and
capacity is one content PR per pass against one content PR a night, so there is slack. It is not
self-healing if the scheduler keeps dropping runs, and in that case nothing merges and the site
simply stops updating with no error anywhere.

**I did not investigate a cause I cannot establish from here**, but one thing is worth putting in
front of you: the cron fires at **minute 0**, the single most contended minute on GitHub's shared
scheduler, and GitHub's own guidance is to avoid it because runs at peak times are delayed or
dropped. Yesterday's report recorded the same shape once ("the 12:00 UTC pass had not landed as of
12:52 UTC"). Three in a row is new.

Two things are immediately available and neither needs code: `merge.yml` carries a
`workflow_dispatch` with an `execute` input, so a pass can be triggered by hand right now; and the
Actions run history will show within the next hour whether the 15:00 UTC slot fires on its own.
