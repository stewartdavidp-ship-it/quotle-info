skeptic pass re-checked three hard findings defaulting to "this finding is wrong"; all three survived,

linked page documents."*

scans of the 1813 T. Egerton first edition**, including the comma after *"fortune"* that the
universally-quoted Project Gutenberg text (the 1894 George Allen edition) drops. Our wording matches
1813; the version most of the internet quotes does not, and both pages now document the split.
