The 251 are the new part. `tools/template.js:272-286` emits `creator` on a disputed page **only**
when it names the true author, or when both the page's author and the record's creator are the
anonymous token. These 251 records put the *magnet* — the wrongly-credited name — in
`schema.creator`, so the guard correctly drops it and the page ships no creator at all. Within them:

- **101** have a visible author that is itself unknown or anonymous (`"No verified author"`,
  `"Origin unknown"`, `"Anonymous (proverbial)"`). These are **mechanically fixable**: write the
  anonymous token into `schema.creator.name` and the generator's existing `creatorIsAnon` arm emits
  an honest `creator` with a `disambiguatingDescription`. No editorial judgement needed.
- **150** name a real person on the page who simply is not in `schema.creator`.

The five `verified` records are the sharpest case — named, undisputed authors whose pages ship no
`Quotation.creator`: **Robert Frost**, **Edith Wharton**, **Jerome K. Jerome**, **Ralph Waldo
Emerson** (all four in the nine-record no-`schema`-block set) and
`a-friend-loveth-at-all-times-and-a-brother-is-born-for` (King James Bible, has a `schema` block, no
`creator` key).

**`tools/template.js:279` sizes this in its own comment as "a CLAUDE.md RULE violation the audit
flagged on ~30 pages". On built output it is 499.** That is the same shape as the finding yesterday's
weekly audit reported — an open decision sized at "2 pages" that measured 406.
