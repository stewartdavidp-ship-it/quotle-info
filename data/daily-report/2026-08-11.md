## Findings

### 1. r35 measured the "Actually by X" defect this series has been carrying since 2026-08-06 — 53 live pages, named

Carried decision item 11 has said for five days that a `disputed` page with nobody else credited
falls through to *"Actually by ⟨the author the page credits⟩"*. A r35 fix agent traced the mechanism
and **counted it**:

> with no top-level `creditedTo`, `primaryCredit()` is empty, `rightPersonWrongWords()` cannot fire,
> and the page falls through to fabrication defaults — **1/"False"** plus *"This attribution is
> disputed. Actually by ⟨the author the page credits⟩."* **The derivation is correct; the data is
> missing.**

> Measured across 1,551 records: 860 disputed → 98 without `creditedTo` → 65 where
> `answer.authorName == schema.creator.name` → **53 (3.4%)** after excluding records with a hand-set
> override.

**I re-derived the first three numbers against the current tree and they match exactly: 860 / 98 /
65.** Named live pages, spot-rendered by that agent:

- `alas-poor-yorick-i-knew-him-well` — *"Actually by William Shakespeare"*, 1/False
- `i-never-said-most-of-the-things-i-said` — same shape, Yogi Berra
- `frankly-scarlett-i-dont-give-a-damn` — *"Actually by Margaret Mitchell"*, 1/False

In its own summary: *"~53 live pages currently tell an answer engine that Shakespeare is falsely
credited with Hamlet."* This is the most serious thing in today's report and it is no longer a
suspicion. **→ decision 1.**
