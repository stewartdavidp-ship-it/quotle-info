### 5.2 NEW — the documented escape hatch for `docLang()` is unreachable from any wave

The wave's own words, reported and deliberately not fixed:

> **`DOSSIER_SCHEMA.source.docMeta` cannot emit `lang`.** `template.js` documents `r.lang` as the
> override for `docLang()`'s label heuristic, but `docMeta` items are `additionalProperties: false`
> with only `{dt, dd, ddClass}` — so the documented escape hatch is unreachable from any wave. A row
> whose *label* names a language while its *value* is English gets mis-tagged (here `lang="zh"` on
> English prose). Worked around per-record by rewording the label. Note the same heuristic feeds
> `isTranslatedSource()`, **which gates a PD-badge caveat — so on a `public-domain` record this
> misfire has teeth, not just an accessibility cost.**

That last sentence is why this is item **97** and not a minor-severity note: the same misfire that
mislabels prose for a screen reader can also suppress or invent a public-domain caveat. Today's
record was `in-copyright`, so nothing false shipped.
