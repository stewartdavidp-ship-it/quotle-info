### 5.3 The refused finding — right call, wrong premise, and the real scope is a mess

The one refusal, in the wave's words:

> **Refused (1), with reasoning:** `creator.birthDate` is `-0497` for Sophocles, but ISO 8601
> astronomical numbering makes 497 BC `-0496`. I did **not** change it. This is the established
> corpus-wide convention (Aesop `-0620`, Aristotle `-0384`, Confucius `-0550`; **1,762 records carry
> a creator birthDate on this pattern**), so correcting one record would make it the inconsistent
> one.

**Measured against the corpus, the parenthesis is wrong and the conclusion it supports does not
hold.** 1,762 records carry a `creator.birthDate`; **47** carry a *negative* one, and only those are
touched by the astronomical-numbering question. Those 47 span 19 distinct `(name, date)` pairs for 16
people:

| birthDate | Creator | Records |
|---|---|---|
| `-0003` / `-0004` | **Seneca the Younger** | 1 / 3 (+1 as "Seneca", `-0004`) |
| `-0427` / `-0428` | **Plato** | 1 / 1 |
| `-0550` / `-0551` / `-0551-09-28` | **Confucius** | 3 / 6 / 2 |
| `-0043` | Ovid | 1 |
| `-0065` | Horace | 1 |
| `-0070` | Virgil | 1 |
| `-0100` | Julius Caesar | 2 |
| `-0106` | Marcus Tullius Cicero | 4 |
| `-0286` | Archimedes | 2 |
| `-0384` | Aristotle | 8 |
| `-0460` | Hippocrates | 3 |
| `-0470` | Socrates | 4 |
| `-0544` | Sun Tzu | 2 |
| `-0620` | Aesop | 1 |

Three consequences the operator needs:

1. **There is no single convention to be consistent with.** Three of the sixteen people already
   disagree with themselves. "Correcting one record would make it the inconsistent one" describes a
   corpus that does not exist.
2. **`-0550` for Confucius — one of the three examples the refusal cites as the convention — is the
   value that is *correct* under astronomical numbering** if the intended year is 551 BC, while the
   same author's other 8 records say `-0551` and `-0551-09-28`. So the cited exemplars are not even
   internally consistent with each other. A blind "subtract one" backfill would therefore *introduce*
   errors as well as fix them; the fix needs the intended BC year per person.
3. **Nothing checks this.** `grep birthDate tools/scan.js tools/verify-corpus.js` returns nothing.
   Three authors carrying contradictory birth dates across their own pages is invisible to the 30
   scan detectors, to the 51 corpus invariants, and to CI. `daily-review` scanned all 2,308 records
   this morning and flagged 0 — correctly, by its own rules.

This is a discrepancy report, not a correction of the wave's judgement. **Refusing a per-record edit
from a content PR was right.** Proposals 1 and 2 in § 6.
