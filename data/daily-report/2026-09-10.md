### 5.3 A generator defect makes the safer rating unreachable — reported by the wave, confirmed here

Today's wave reported this rather than editing `tools/`, and was explicit about staying in its lane
(*"`git status --porcelain -- tools workflows` is clean on this branch"*). In its words:

> `rightPersonWrongWords()` (`template.js:2219`) requires a `creditedTo` magnet **equal to** the hero.
> `prep-wave.js:207` stamps `creditedTo` **only when** hero ≠ magnet. Those conditions are mutually
> exclusive, so the derived 2/"Mostly False" rating can never fire on a wave-built
> right-person-wrong-words record — it lands on 1/"False", the rating `template.js` itself calls
> libellous for that shape. 152 corpus records are currently rating-2-eligible, all via `creditedTo`
> values that predate or bypass that guard.

**I read both sites and the claim holds.** `template.js:2226` returns false unless
`heroName.toLowerCase() === magnetName`; `prep-wave.js:207` assigns `rec.creditedTo = b.author` only
`if (leadName(rec.answer.authorName) !== leadName(b.author))`. There is no path through both.

This is not theoretical — it fired today, as the wave's one high finding, on the Jane Austen record:

> The Austen page emitted `ClaimReview.reviewRating` = 1/"False" while the *same page* asserts
> `Quotation.creator` = Jane Austen with `disambiguatingDescription` reading "Jane Austen is the
> author, but this popular wording is not what they wrote". `tools/template.js:469-475` says 1/5
> "genuinely libels" exactly this shape.

The wave fixed its own record by hand (`schema.claimRatingValue = 2`). Every future wave hits the same
wall. **New item 76.**
