## 5. Findings

### 5.1 232 pages show one wording and tell machines another — item 47, finally measured

This is new, and it is the largest published defect this report has been able to put a number to.

`displayQuote` is the visible quotation: it is the page's `<h1>`, its `<title>`, and its
`meta.title`. **232 records on `main` carry a Title-Cased `displayQuote`** — Quote Investigator's
article-headline casing — and the pattern is unmistakable in the sample:

```
[disputed] Life Is Just One Damn Thing After Another
[disputed] Whiskey Is for Drinking; Water Is for Fighting Over
[verified] So I'm Ugly. So What? I Never Saw Anyone Hit with His Face
[disputed] An Exclamation Point Is Like Laughing at Your Own Joke
```

Measured against each page's own JSON-LD `Quotation.text`, **171 records are word-for-word identical
and differ only in case and punctuation.** So the page asserts one rendering of the quotation to a
reader and a different one to a machine. Today's wave ships three more of exactly this shape in
#1119, and the divergence there is visible in a single record:

```
displayQuote : I Only Write When Inspiration Strikes. Fortunately It Strikes at Nine Every Morning
meta.title   : Who really said "I Only Write When Inspiration Strikes. ..."? | Quotle.info
JSON-LD text : I only write when inspiration strikes. Fortunately it strikes at nine every morning.
```

On the Kennedy record in the same PR the visible form is also **truncated** relative to the
structured data — `If Men Could Get Pregnant, Abortion Would Be a Sacrament` against
`Honey, if men could get pregnant, abortion would be a sacrament.`

**One correction to a measurement, so nobody repeats it:** a first pass found 885 records where
`displayQuote` and `Quotation.text` differ in *words*, which looks alarming and is not a defect. Those
are the design — the visible line is the short circulating form, the structured text the fuller
verbatim passage (*"the fuller wording is restored and the dropped clause shown"*, #670's own words).
**Only the 171 case-only divergences are the defect**, and the 232 Title-Cased records are where they
come from.
