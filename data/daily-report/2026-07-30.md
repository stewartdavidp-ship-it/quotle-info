## Findings

### 1. The 07:00 merge pass did not run, and that is what broke the night

It is the only authority that merges routine PRs. Without it: #267 and #268 went `BEHIND` when #269
landed, and #266 went from mergeable to **conflicted**. `merge-gate.js` right now reports
`0 to merge, 2 needing a rebase+rebuild, 1 left alone`. Nothing self-heals — the pass is also the
only thing that runs the `REBUILD` route.

The pass is a **local** routine (`workflows/DAILY-MERGE.md`: "A local routine runs this every day at
07:00 ET"), so a laptop that was asleep or a routine that is no longer armed both look like this. That
is the thing to check first.

### 2. 431 pages — 34.5% of the site — tell readers the source could not be verified, on pages that document it

Reported by the r28 wave (#269) as a generator-tier defect and deliberately **not fixed inside a
content wave**. Its words:

> `renderPresentationKit` (`tools/template.js:979`) falls back to *"We couldn't verify where this
> wording comes from, so there's no rights status to give — just don't assert a specific author or
> source"* whenever a record has neither `source.rights` nor `source.useLine`. All 431 such records
> **do** document a source, and the line renders directly beneath a credit block telling the reader to
> copy that exact author and source. Verified at the rendered layer, not inferred: 431 of 431 built
> pages contain the string. The root cause is a conflation — omitting `rights` means *"rights
> uncertain"* (per `validate-records.js`), but the fallback reads it as *"attribution unverified"*.
> Those are different claims and only one of them is true. The fallback should speak to rights only.

**This is live on the published site**, and it is the same class as #256/#259/#263 — a page asserting
something the pipeline cannot back. It is the most serious content finding in the report.

### 3. The reader-reports pass cannot run at all from the cloud container

#267, in full: it "stopped at step 1", because `GET /reports/pending` returned 403 — and

> **That 403 is not the origin's.** It is the agent proxy in this cloud container refusing the CONNECT
> tunnel. […] `quotle.info` is blocked the same way; the four citation-source hosts are not. So the
> block is per-host egress policy for this session, not a site outage.

It is explicit that this is **not** a site outage and must not be read as one:

> the endpoint's real status is unknown from this container and is **NOT confirmed down.** Anyone
> reading this PR should check the worker from an unblocked network before concluding anything about
> it.

Consequences, in its own terms: "Steps 2–6 […] **not run.**" and "**Nothing was stamped, so no report
was closed and no reader was emailed.**" The queue is untouched and resurfaces next pass. Correctly,
it did not retry or route around the denial.

**And preflight cannot see it:**

> `preflight.js --routine reports` passed all 13 checks *immediately before* this failure, and
> reported "safe to proceed". It probes the four hosts the corpus cites […] but never `QUOTLE_API`
> (`tools/review.js:40`), the single host this pass cannot run without. […] A
> `reach quotle-community.stewartd.workers.dev` check would have turned this run into a one-line stop
> instead of a diagnosis.

Reported not fixed, per the generator-tier rule. It is a one-line addition to the host list.

### 4. #266 is conflicted, and two waves raced for the same base

The scheduled 03:00 cloud wave (#266, `wave-d20260730`, 5 records, corpus 1209 → 1214) and the
operator's r28 wave (#269, 40 records, corpus 1209 → **1249**) branched from the same `main`
(`4409262a`) and both rewrote `data/corpus-state.json` and the backlog. #269 merged first, so #266 now
conflicts. `DAILY-MERGE.md` prescribes the fix and forbids hand-resolving built output — take `main`'s
tree, keep `data/quotes` from the branch, rebuild.

**Nothing bad is live because of this.** #266's blocker fix is on a page that has never published —
confirmed, `data/quotes/so-it-goes.json` and `who-said/so-it-goes/` do not exist on `main`. The wave's
own account of it:

> The "So it goes" page listed "a hundred thousand civilians" among the deaths the refrain marks. No
> cited source on the page carries any Dresden casualty figure, and the number is one the historical
> consensus rejects […]. An unsourced casualty figure on a page about the bombing of Dresden is a
> published factual error. Removed.

The cost of the conflict is only that the 5 records — and a genuinely good find, that Emerson's "hitch
your wagon to a star" is in print in *The Atlantic Monthly* for **April 1862**, eight years before the
1870 book every quote site cites — do not publish.

#266 also reports a generator defect it refused to patch: the `ClaimReview` on the fire-engine page
"asserts `claimReviewed: 'Kurt Vonnegut said: …'` and rates it Verified — on a page whose whole point
is that Vonnegut *wrote* the line and a *character* speaks it." The string is composed by the
generator, so no record edit can reach it.

### 5. `daily-report.js` reports every routine that ran as "did not run" whenever the merge pass is behind

Detailed above. The tool's own header says its purpose is that "a routine that did not run at all and

that never fired *are* identical to it until the merge pass lands the shard. Today three routines were
misreported. This is the second consecutive morning the reporting tool itself produced the report's
lead finding (yesterday: undercounted wave runs, fixed in #251).

### 6. `reports-close` was flagged "DID NOT RUN" four hours before it is due

The tool compares against the expected roster without comparing the scheduled time to now. At 08:13 ET

### 7. Two generator defects that block the `isPartOf` backfill recommended on #264

From #269, reported not applied:

> **`schema.isPartOf` still cannot carry a citation.** The `isPartOf` block (`template.js:281-290`)
> reads only `type/name/datePublished/sameAs/inLanguage`, while the sibling `isBasedOn` block at
> `291-303` honours `.citation` and `.description`. So a record moving its carrier from `isBasedOn` to
> `isPartOf` — exactly the migration recommended on #264 — **silently loses the ability to cite the
> fact-check that documents it.** Mirror lines 302-303 into the `isPartOf` branch before running that
> backfill, or the backfill degrades those records.

And separately: "**`ClaimReview` has no `citation` passthrough.** […] An opt-in
`schema.claimCitation` hook would let the debunking work sit on the review."

Scope, measured by that wave: "1,159 of 1,249 records have no `schema.isPartOf`, 1,017 carry
`isBasedOn` without it, and 1,001 of those show no translation signal — a mechanical rename."
