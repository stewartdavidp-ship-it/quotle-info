## 5. Findings

### 5.1 Three pages currently serve a `Quotation.creator` their own JSON-LD node denies — fix is unmerged

`weekly-discovery` (#1015) found the class and named the gap in its own words:

> "The generator already guards this: on a `disputed` page it emits `creator` only when it matches
> `answer.authorName` and that hero is not the magnet. … **What the guard cannot see is a record that
> put a reporter in the hero slot.** Then creator matches the hero, the hero is not the magnet, every
> test passes, and a false authorship claim ships."

I confirmed all three are **live on `main` right now**, reading the built pages directly:

| page | `creator` | `disambiguatingDescription` on the **same node** |
|---|---|---|
| `let-us-hope-darwinism-…` | **Robert Forman Horton** | "…The earliest documented appearance is R. F. Horton's *Verbum Dei*…" |
| `we-can-complain-because-rose-bushes-…` | **Alphonse Karr** | "**No originator is known**: the oldest documented version is a French quatrain Alphonse Karr printed in 1853…" |
| `who-is-buried-in-grants-tomb-…` | **Ed Wynn** | "**The originator is unknown**: Ed Wynn's column…" |

Discovery went to the primary source on the first: Horton's *Verbum Dei* (1893), p. 132, on the
archive.org scan, has him **quoting** "that timid and decorous spinster who … said, 'Let us hope it is
not true, or if it is, let us hush it up.'" Its verdictNote already said Horton is *"the earliest
documented teller, **not an established author of the saying**"* — so, in discovery's words, **"one node
asserted and denied the same authorship."**

All three are fixed in **#1015**, which is `MERGE`-ready and unmerged. This is the concrete cost of §2.

### 5.2 The detector for it was measured `BACKFILL` and correctly **rejected**

`propose-detector.js` scored `creator-contradicts-verdict-note` at **15 hits, 0.7%, verdict BACKFILL**.
Discovery declined it and said why:

> "Of five hits inspected, three are false positives in which the credited person genuinely **said** the
> words and merely did not coin them. … The real distinction is whether the person **uttered the words at
