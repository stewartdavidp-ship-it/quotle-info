### 5.5 Generator defect reported, not patched — new item 103

> `tools/mis-kind.js` under-marks a row that affirms the true author when its tag is the literal
> string `genuine`. `kindForRow()` matches `AFFIRMS_TRUTH` on a scope like "The real author" and then,
> inside that branch, consults `kindForTag()`, where `/^genuine$/i` (added for rows whose `who` is the
> page's own author) returns `context`. So the affirming branch returns the neutral tilde instead of
> the sage ✓.

It **hit both true-author rows in this wave** (Victor Hugo, Sam Levenson), was worked around
per-record by dropping the redundant tag, and `tools/` was left untouched — *"the central fix is
presumably to let an affirming scope win over that one tag pattern, and it wants its own branch and
its own measured pass over the corpus."*

This is the **third distinct `mis-kind.js` defect** in the open ledger (items 61, 96, now 103). The
corpus currently carries `genuine=129` misattribution rows, which bounds the sweep.
