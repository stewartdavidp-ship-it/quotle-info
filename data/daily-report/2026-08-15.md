### 5. An audit finding escalated centrally — but its scale claim does not match the corpus

The wave refused a per-record patch and asked for a central decision:

> *"On `seventy-two-suburbs`, `Quotation.creator` is `{name: "Unknown"}` with `sameAs` pointing at
> Parker's Wikiquote page — machine-readably close to equating the unknown author *with* Parker. Not
> changed: this is corpus-wide convention across ~255 `Unknown`-author records […] Diverging one
> record from its 255 siblings is worse than the nit. **This wants a central decision, not a
> per-record patch.**"*

The concern is real and I confirmed the specific case (`sameAs` →
`en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Dorothy_Parker#Misattributed`). **The count is not.** Measured on `main`
(1,567 records, `schema.creator.name === "Unknown"`):

- **97** records have an `Unknown` creator — not ~255.
- **80** of those carry a `sameAs` at all.
- Of those 80, roughly **24** point at a *named person's* Wikiquote/Wikipedia page — the conflation
  case the wave describes (Confucius, Orwell, Einstein, Marilyn Monroe, Rockefeller, Nietzsche…).
- The other **~53** point at *evidence* articles — Quote Investigator (27), Snopes, Barry Popik,
  loc.gov — where there is no conflation risk at all, because the target is not a person.

This matters for the decision: the wave declined on the grounds that the fix would diverge one record
from 255 siblings. The real blast radius is about **24 records**, and the other 53 need no change —
which makes this considerably more tractable than the PR body implies.
