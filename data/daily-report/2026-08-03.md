### 3. Discovery: the site ships German and French prose declared as English to machines

The strongest finding of the night, from #305. `template.js:212-216` hardcodes
`quotation.inLanguage = 'en'` on a premise the audit measured as false:

> **That premise is false for 8 records.** `Quotation.text` takes `schema.quotationText`, and some
> records put the *original-language* sentence there — so the site ships German prose declared as
> English in the machine-readable layer it exists to serve.

All eight hand-checked, 8/8 genuinely non-English — `ich-bin-ein-berliner`,
`chaos-ist-die-ordnung-des-genies`, `history-is-but-a-fable-agreed-upon` (French) and five others.
The detector is written and passed the gate (8 hits, 0.6%) but **was not admitted** — admission is
deliberately serial — and the real fix is in `template.js`, out of a discovery pass's lane.

A second detector passed too (`lastverified-dateModified-mismatch`, 3 hits, 0.2%: the visible "Last
verified" date newer than `schema.dateModified` on 2 of 3). A third was **rejected with its
measurement recorded** — 46 hits, precision 0/4, because every hit was a disputed record where the
"we couldn't establish where this was first published" line is correct. That rejection is the gate
doing its job.
