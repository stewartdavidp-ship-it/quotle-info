## Findings

### 1. NEW — `creditLine()` strips the correction, then `verbatimNote()` declines to fire *because* it was stripped. 75 records.

Today's wave reported it and deliberately did not patch it. In its words:

> `creditLine()` (~L1091) **strips** a leading quoted segment from `copyAttribution`.
> `verbatimNote()` (~L1127) suppresses itself when the record looks "already corrected by hand" — but
> it tests the **unstripped** `copyAttribution`. … Both functions are individually correct; the
> interaction is the bug. This is the Shaw failure documented in the comment block directly above
> `verbatimNote`, reintroduced by a different route.

**Verified in the tree**, both halves: `creditLine` (`tools/template.js:1092`) applies
`.replace(/^\s*["“][^"”]*["”][.,]?\s*[—–-]?\s*/, '')`, while `verbatimNote`
(`tools/template.js:1128`) computes its guard from `q.copyAttribution` raw.

**The wave hit it on 4 of 5 records; across the merged corpus it is 75.** Spot-rendered
`a-rose-by-any-other-name-would-smell-as-sweet`: `copyAttribution` opens *"What's in a name? That
which we call a rose / By any other name would smell as sweet."* — and the built page's kit contains
that string **zero** times and no `Verbatim:` note. The paste-ready deliverable ships the clipping
the page exists to correct.

The wave's suggested fix is one line — *"have the guard test `creditLine(q)` rather than raw
`q.copyAttribution`"* — and it applied a **record-level workaround to its own 4** (tail-only
`copyAttribution`), so #455 is not waiting on this. **→ decision 1.**
