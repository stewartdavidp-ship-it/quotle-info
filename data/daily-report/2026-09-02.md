### 5.2 The daily wave fixed a false public-domain claim on the one page badged public-domain

#742 built five records, all **disputed and all genuine reassignments** away from the magnet author
(Groucho→Unknown 1935, Thoreau→Taylor Caldwell 1968, Huxley→Unknown 1860, Huxley→Ray Manzarek 1967,

> The Shakespeare record's `rightsNote` said Huxley's 1925 *Those Barren Leaves* wording "belongs to
