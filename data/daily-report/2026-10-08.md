## 5. Findings

### 5.1 A page published "the true author is not established" against its own verified verdict — and nothing in the repo catches it

This is the night's most serious finding and the wave reported it rather than quietly fixing it. On
`a-master-in-the-art-of-living-makes-little-distinction`, the rendered JSON-LD omitted
`Quotation.creator` and published, in the wave's words:

> `"disambiguatingDescription": "Commonly misattributed to James Michener. The true author is not established; no creator is asserted here."`

> That is a machine-readable statement **contradicting the page's own primary-verified verdict**, and
> it dropped the `creator` the CLAUDE.md RULE requires on every quote page.

The cause is not a bug in the guard. `tools/template.js`'s `creatorOk` (line 283) emits `creator` on a
`disputed` record only when `schema.creator.name` equals `answer.authorName` **exactly**; the record
said `Lawrence Pearsall Jacks` while the hero was `L. P. Jacks`. The wave's judgement:

> The guard is correct and deliberate (it stops the magnet being credited); the record was wrong.

It fixed its own record, and reported the gap it did not fix:

> **Central gap, reported not fixed** (no `tools/` or `workflows/` changes in this PR): nothing gates
> this. `validate-records.js` and `detectors.js` contain no check on `creator.name` vs
> `answer.authorName`, so a wave can silently ship a page asserting its own author is unknown.

**I checked this measurement independently, because it is the basis of proposal 1.** Replicating
`creatorOk` with `template.js`'s own exported `plain()` and `rightPersonWrongWords()` and
`credits.js`'s `primaryCredit()` over `main`'s 1,295 `disputed` records:

| | count | guard's behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| creator emitted | 802 | — |
| **named hero, one name contains the other** | **61** | **unintended — the Jacks shape** |
| named hero, genuinely different names | 42 | probably correct |
| hero unknown, creator not anonymous | 100 | correct (refuses to credit the magnet) |
| hero == magnet, not right-person-wrong-words | 48 | correct (refuses to assert the misattribution) |
| no `schema.creator` in the record at all | 242 | not the guard — a data gap |

**The wave's "61 short/long-form variants" is exact.** Its 43 genuinely-different reads 42 here, a
one-record difference explained by its measuring on its own branch after fixing Jacks. Its "104" is
61 + 43 — the named-hero cases only, which is what it described.

Examples of the 61, all live on the site now:

- `a-picture-is-worth-a-thousand-words` — creator `Arthur Brisbane`, hero
  `Arthur Brisbane (popularized by Fred R. Barnard)`
- `a-verbal-contract-isnt-worth-the-paper-its-written-on` — creator `Bryan O'Loghlen`, hero
  `Sir Bryan O'Loghlen`
- `an-army-of-sheep-led-by-a-lion-is-better-than-an-army-of` — creator `Chabrias`, hero
  `Chabrias (as recorded by Plutarch)`
- `be-who-you-are-and-say-what-you-feel-because-those-who-mind` — creator `Bernard M. Baruch`, hero
  `Bernard M. Baruch (for the punchline only)`

In every one the record *knows* the author and the page refuses to say so in JSON-LD. The hero field
is carrying an editorial gloss in a field the guard compares byte-for-byte.

**A second number the wave did not report, and which no open proposal covers:** measured against the
built pages rather than the records, **493 of 1,295 `disputed` pages ship no `Quotation.creator`** —
the 251 suppressed above plus **242 records that carry no `creator` field at all**. The RULE asks for
a creator on every quote page. 242 records cannot satisfy it regardless of what the guard does.
Proposal 2.
