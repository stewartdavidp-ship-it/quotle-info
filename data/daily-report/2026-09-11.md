### 5.2 New item 77 — `meta.description` escapes markup no gate can see

The wave reported this rather than fixing it (nothing under `tools/` is touched in #849):

> "**`meta.description` / `meta.ogDescription` are not in `tools/html-safety.js` `PLAIN_TEXT_FIELDS`.**
> They render through `esc()` into an attribute, so any inline markup ships as literal `&lt;em&gt;` in
> the search snippet — and neither `prep-wave.js`'s plain-text scrub nor `validate-records.js` catches
> it, because both key off that same list. This wave hit it twice in five records."

**Confirmed in the code.** `tools/html-safety.js:107-114` lists twelve fields; `meta.description` and
`meta.ogDescription` are not among them, and `stripPlainTextMarkup()` (line 136) iterates exactly that
list. So the scrub is a no-op on the two fields most likely to be hand-written with emphasis.

This is not new behaviour — **#670 fixed the same defect by hand on 2026-08-27** ("Escaped `<em>` tags
in **three** meta descriptions"), and today's audit caught it twice more. Three waves, five records,
one two-line fix never made. The wave is right that it "would have made it a no-op at prep time", and
right that existing pages want a corpus-wide sweep.
