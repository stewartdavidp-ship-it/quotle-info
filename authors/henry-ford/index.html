Henry Ford

1863–1947 · American industrialist

Henry Ford (1863–1947) founded the Ford Motor Company in 1903 and, with the moving assembly line and the Model T, brought the automobile within reach of ordinary Americans. A relentless, hands-on problem-solver, he cultivated a public image as a plain-spoken tinkerer — which is exactly why brisk, fix-it aphorisms like this one attach so easily to his name. Many sayings pinned to Ford, however, cannot be traced to anything he actually wrote or said.
