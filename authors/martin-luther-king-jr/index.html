Martin Luther King Jr.

1929–1968 · Baptist minister and leader of the American civil rights movement

Martin Luther King Jr. was an American Baptist minister and the most prominent leader of the civil rights movement from 1955 until his assassination in 1968, advancing civil rights through nonviolent resistance and civil disobedience against Jim Crow laws and other forms of legalised discrimination. Stride Toward Freedom: The Montgomery Story (1958) was his first book, an account of the Montgomery bus boycott that doubled as his first full statement of the philosophy behind it. Because his name is among the most quoted in English, it is also among the most falsely quoted — which makes the genuinely sourced lines, like this one, worth marking as sourced.
