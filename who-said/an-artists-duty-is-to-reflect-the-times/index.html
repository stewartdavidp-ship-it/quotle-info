An artist's duty is to reflect the times

Spoken by Nina Simone 1933–2003 · Singer, pianist & civil-rights activist
✓ Attribution status: Verified ©Reuse status: In copyright

This one is real. Nina Simone said it on camera in a televised interview (archival footage from around 1969), the clip most people now know from the 2015 documentary What Happened, Miss Simone?. Her full line kept a qualifier the meme drops: “An artist's duty, as far as I'm concerned, is to reflect the times.”

Context

Why it mattered

Simone offered this definition of the artist at the height of the U.S. civil-rights struggle, a period when she had turned her performances into acts of protest with songs like Mississippi Goddam. For her, reflecting the times was not optional decoration but the very thing that made someone an artist.

That is why the line resonates so widely today: it reframes political and social engagement as the artist's core responsibility rather than a distraction from craft.

About Nina Simone

NS

Nina Simone

1933–2003 · Singer, pianist & civil-rights activist

Nina Simone, born Eunice Kathleen Waymon, was an American singer, pianist, and songwriter whose work moved freely across jazz, blues, soul, gospel, and classical music. A central artistic voice of the civil-rights movement, she wrote and performed protest songs such as Mississippi Goddam and To Be Young, Gifted and Black, and came to be called the “High Priestess of Soul.”
