Who really said it

The way to get started is to quit talking and begin doing

Attributed to Walt Disney 1901–1966 · American animator & film producer
≈ Attribution status: Attributed

Widely — and, unusually for a feel-good internet quote, correctly — credited to Disney. It traces to a July 1957 interview with Hollywood columnist Hedda Hopper, in which Disney said “I found the way to get started was to quit talking and begin doing it” while describing how he pressed ahead without bank financing. The famous one-liner is a lightly trimmed paraphrase of that documented statement, so the attribution is sound even though the exact popular wording is not verbatim.

Fact-check

What actually happened

This is not a case of the wrong person — Disney really did say essentially this. The distortion is subtler: the wording was smoothed and the original meaning stripped away.

✕
WordingWalt Disney (paraphrased) trimmed paraphrase

Disney’s documented words were first-person and past-tense — “I found the way to get started was to quit talking and begin doing it” — per Snopes ↗. The meme form recasts it as a timeless imperative, dropping “I found” and “it” and shifting to the present tense.

✕
ContextMotivational reposts missing context

Disney was describing how he pushed his ventures forward without waiting for bank credit, not offering generic life advice; Snopes ↗ notes this financing context is absent from modern uses.

✓ The attribution is sound: Disney genuinely voiced this idea in a July 1957 Hedda Hopper interview ↗ — the popular version just tidies his words and forgets he was talking about money.

Context

Why it resonates

The line endures because it compresses Disney’s entire biography — a career built on launching before the money, the studio, or the certainty was in place — into six blunt words. Stripped of its 1957 banking context, it reads as universal permission to stop deliberating and act.

That universality is also why it travels so well and why the original financing story vanished: the phrase works as pure motivation without it.

Quit talking and begin doing.

On the wording drift

Disney’s recorded statement — “I didn’t have the money to get this thing going, but I found the way to get started was to quit talking and begin doing it” — is a memory of a specific business decision. The aphorism it spawned keeps the punch and discards the particulars, which is typical of how first-person anecdotes harden into quotable maxims.

About Walt Disney

WD

Walt Disney

1901–1966 · American animator, film producer & studio co-founder

Walt Disney (1901–1966) was an American animator, film producer, and entrepreneur who co-founded The Walt Disney Company with his brother Roy in 1923. He pioneered the animated feature with Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs (1937), created Mickey Mouse, opened Disneyland in 1955, and won a record 22 competitive Academy Awards. His early career was marked by financial struggle and gambles made without secured funding — the very experience behind this quote.
